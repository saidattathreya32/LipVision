1. config.py -----------> Sets configuration, vocabulary, and thresholds
2. data_processor.py ---> Extracts 3D mesh points & normalizes lip motion
3. collect_data.py -----> Records video clips & creates features.npy & labels.npy
4. model.py ------------> PyTorch TCN-BiGRU Network Architecture
5. train.py ------------> Trains model and saves lipread_v3_best.pth & lipread_v3.onnx
6. predict_live.py -----> Runs real-time lip-reading from your webcam
